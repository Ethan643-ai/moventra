Confirmed baseline · Person No. 1

Current Process (Discharge → Gate-Out)

This is our confirmed operational baseline from port operations — we did not invent it; it was described by someone who runs the process.
  1. 1
    DischargeContainer unloaded from vessel into port yard or ICD.
  2. 2
    Customs DeclarationClearing agent lodges documents and files the customs declaration (TRA).
  3. 3
    Customs / Agency InspectionScanner and multi-agency checks (e.g. TBS, TMDA, Government Chemist).
  4. 4
    Customs ReleaseDuties paid · TRA issues Customs Release / release order.
  5. 5
    Shipping Line / Delivery Order (DO)Ocean freight settled · DO issued by the shipping line.
  6. 6
    TPA Charges & ReleasePort storage & handling paid · TPA release + EIR.
  7. 7
    Gate BookingTruck plate & driver linked in the TPA gate-pass system.
  8. 8
    Yard LoadingContainer physically loaded onto the assigned truck in the yard.
  9. 9
    Gate-OutFinal seal/document check · truck exits the port gate.

A delay after customs clearance is not always a TRA problem. Person No. 1 mapped this sequence:
DO expired / blocked→ Storage / demurrage unpaid→ Gate Pass issue→ Truck / driver mismatch→ Yard blockage
“Where is your container stuck, why, and what is the next action?” — instead of calling five different offices.

1 · Executive Summary

MOVENTRA is a logistics technology company building a Digital Freight Operating System for freight movement across East Africa. The platform connects cargo owners, transport companies, truck owners, clearing and forwarding agents, ports, financial institutions, insurance providers, and other logistics stakeholders through one intelligent system.

The logistics sector in East Africa remains highly fragmented. Many transactions still depend on phone calls, WhatsApp messages, manual coordination, paper-based processes, and disconnected service providers. That creates inefficiencies, delays, limited transparency, trust gaps, and avoidable costs across the freight value chain.

MOVENTRA addresses these challenges by creating a digital marketplace and operating layer for freight logistics: request freight services, discover verified transport providers, track shipments, manage logistics documentation, coordinate payments, and gain operational visibility through data-driven tools. The long-term ambition is to become the digital backbone of freight movement in East Africa, and a scalable logistics infrastructure platform for broader African trade corridors.
